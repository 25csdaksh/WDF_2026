Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find quick answers to common queries regarding portal access, attendance tracking, event registrations, and examinations.

1. How do I register as a new student on StudentHub?

Navigate to the Registration Page, input your institutional enrollment number, department, semester, and university email. Upon verification, your account will be activated.

2. What should I do if I forget my portal password?

You can reset your password by reaching out to the IT Helpdesk via the Contact Page or contacting your department lab administrator.

3. What is the minimum attendance requirement for semester exams?

As per university guidelines, students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance across all registered theory and laboratory courses to be eligible for mid-sem and end-sem examinations.

4. How can I register for campus hackathons and workshops?

Visit the Campus Events Page, browse the active events, and click the "Register for Event" button associated with your preferred event.

5. How do I provide feedback about course delivery or portal usability?

You can submit direct and structured feedback anonymously or with credentials via the Feedback Form.
